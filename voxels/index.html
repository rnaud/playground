Voxel Playground

← compare: sprite stacking version

The same world and the same models as the sprite stacking page, but every filled cell is a real cube. Each model is meshed once into triangles (only the exposed faces) and drawn with WebGL, a depth buffer and per-face lighting — at the same low resolution, so the pixels stay chunky.

Scene

WASD / arrows drive · QE or drag to orbit the camera · Drag the scene sideways to orbit (or to look around on foot) · the car self-drives until you take over · Get out (or F) to walk in first person, then walk up to any car and Get in.

Anatomy

Top-down pans in whole-pixel steps for a crisp, steady image; Smooth edges supersamples it instead. On foot is always supersampled.
