A world from code

A house, its furniture and the land around it. Every object is a Sand3D program, run as Python in your browser and meshed live: nothing here was stored as a 3D model.

The first visit builds for about a minute while you walk; after that it loads from your browser's cache.

Credits

Objects are programs from the Sand3D datasets by Sandflow on Hugging Face, CC BY 4.0, rebuilt here by a browser port of the sand3d library (Apache-2.0). The house shell, terrain and pond are made for this page.
