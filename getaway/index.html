Voxel Getaway

← voxel playground

An original top-down city sandbox built on the voxel renderer. Take any car, run jobs for the four contacts, and keep the police off your tail.

▲

WASD drive or walk · F get in / out of the nearest car · on foot Space attacks and 1–4 pick a weapon · V top-down / first person · QE rotate the camera · drag the scene to rotate (or look around in first person).

  • Dispatch — courier runs against the clock
  • Cab Co — pick up a fare and drop them off
  • The Crew — wait at the bank, then lose the police
  • Chop Shop — bring the kind of car they ask for
  • Respray — $150 repairs the car and clears your wanted level
  • The Fixer — clear out the Dockers gang's lookouts
  • Gun shop — pistol $200, shotgun $500, SMG $800, then ammo
  • Hospital — $50 to patch you up; you wake here if you go down
  • Goal — finish five jobs and The Crew offers The Big One
  • Wanted — taking occupied cars, hitting traffic and shooting raise it; at two stars officers get out and open fire; stay away from police to cool down
