Sprite Stacking Playground

Every object below is a pile of flat pixel-art slices (horizontal cross-sections). Each frame, every slice is rotated in 2D and drawn one pixel-row higher than the last. No 3D engine, no meshes, no voxels at render time — just drawImage in a loop.
